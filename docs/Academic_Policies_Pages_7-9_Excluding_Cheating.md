# Academic Policies Manual for BSN Students

## Inappropriate Behavior / Misconduct and Sanction/Extension Duty

### 1. Tardiness

| Inappropriate Behavior / Misconduct | Sanction / Extension Duty |
|---|---|
| a. 15 minutes | a. **1 day** |
| b. 30 minutes above — student is considered absent | b. **3 days** |

**Note:** Students who report late for more than 15 minutes but less than 30 minutes will receive the disciplinary measure of one day extension. The extra number of minutes will be added to any future tardiness incurred.

### 2. Going out of or leaving class/duty without CI approval.

**Sanction / Extension Duty:** **3 days**

### 3. Absence

| Inappropriate Behavior / Misconduct | Sanction / Extension Duty |
|---|---|
| a. Excused absence | a. **Replacement (hrs missed)** |
| b. Unexcused absence | b. **3 days** |

### 4. Entertaining visitors unnecessarily

**Sanction / Extension Duty:** **3 days**

### 5. Loitering in the clinical area

**Sanction / Extension Duty:** **3 days**

### 6. Reading non-professional magazines and other resources while on class/duty

**Sanction / Extension Duty:** **3 days**

### 7. Sleeping during class/duty

**Sanction / Extension Duty:** **1 day**

### 8. Dishonesty

**Sanction / Extension Duty:** **5 days**

### 9. Leaving personal belongings in the classroom/clinical area after class/duty

**Sanction / Extension Duty:** **1 day**

### 10. Not wearing the prescribed uniform

**Sanction / Extension Duty:** **1 day**

### 11. Improper grooming

**Male:** hair should not touch the earlobe, short nails, no makeup, if with contact lens for refractory problems, contact lens should either be clear or black, no jewelry except wristwatch, hair color within the black or brown palette.

**Female:** hair tied in a bun with hairnet, for short-haired females, hair should not touch the uniform collar; short nails without manicure, light makeup, if with contact lens for refractory problems, contact lens should either be clear or black, no jewelry except wristwatch, hair color within the black or brown palette.

**Sanction / Extension Duty:** **1 day + confiscation of jewelry**, to be returned after submission of an explanation letter approved by the confiscating CI and RLE/Academic Coordinator

### 12. Not following CI instruction/direction

**Sanction / Extension Duty:** **2 days**

### 13. Gross insubordination

**Sanction / Extension Duty:** **10 days**

### 14. Use of cellphone, laptop and other electronic gadgets, unless allowed by the Clinical Instructor.

**Sanction / Extension Duty:** **2 days + confiscation of gadget**, to be returned after submission of an explanation letter approved by the confiscating CI and RLE/Academic Coordinator

### 15. Failure to submit complete clinical requirements

**Sanction / Extension Duty:** **1 day per requirement**

### 16. Incomplete ward/CHN kit

**Sanction / Extension Duty:** **1 day for every missing item**

### 17. Failure to attend orientation, evaluation, scheduled conference and other activities

**Sanction / Extension Duty:** **1 day**

### 18. Failure to maintain cleanliness and orderliness of classrooms/student lounge and all other identified learning areas.

**Sanction / Extension Duty:** **2 days**

> **Note:** One day sanction is considered as eight hours of clinical/office duty. Sanctions for offenses made during Lecture hours shall be served as office duty, while sanctions for offenses made during RLE hours shall be served as clinical duty.

## Merit System

A merit system for students whose acts highly display the faculty's and university's core values shall be adopted by the Faculty of Nursing. It shall be according to the following guidelines:

1. Merits shall be in the form of hours or days;
2. A merit is distinctively given to a student and shall be non-transferable;
3. Merits may be given for voluntary work of BSN students during activities of the Faculty and non-compulsory activities of the University;
4. Merits may also be given to students whose outstanding academic and extra-curricular performance contributes to the achievements and gives distinctions to the Faculty of Nursing;
5. A 1:1 system for work-merit ratio shall be adopted. The number of hours/days of voluntary work rendered by the student shall be the corresponding merit hours/days.
6. Merits may be given solely by clinical instructors. The FON Supreme Student Council may recommend merits to the Dean through the Council Adviser;
7. Except for absences incurred during clinical rotation, merits may be used to offset sanctions and extension duties obtained. A 1:1 system for merit-sanction offset ratio shall be adopted;
8. A system for the monitoring of merit imposition and utilization shall be established;
9. A merit is valid for one calendar year.
